Our House

Restaurant manners. Home hospitality.

The Supper Room is our dining room, turned restaurant for the night. We cook a set tasting menu that follows the season, set one long table, light the candles and take care of everything else.

It’s for friends and the friends they bring. There’s no check at the end — just come hungry and stay late.

— Brandon & Kate, your hosts
